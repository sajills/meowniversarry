# Meowniversary

A tiny anniversary website for my girlfriend, because apparently saying "love you" once was not gonna cut it.

First, some notification. Then a chat. Then an envelope, a letter, falling petals, and a soundtrack. Basically, I made a whole side quest just to say "I love you." Worth it, eh?

## Made for Your Phone

It is phone-first, with a portrait layout, touch-friendly bits, and spacing that fits a mobile screen. Desktop and tablet work too, but the phone is where the plot hits. Also, you probably have your phone on you anyway.

## Give It a Go

Double-click `start-local.bat`, then visit `http://localhost:8000`. This serves the site so the browser can load the music; opening `index.html` directly can block the MP3.

## The Files, Eh

- `index.html`: the page structure. The stage is set.
- `style.css`: the layout, look, and animations. Very important for the emotional impact.
- `script.js`: notification timing, chat, envelope, and letter stuff.
- `photos/`: the pictures.
- `audio/everything.mp3`: the soundtrack, cued after the date appears. Main-character moment, basically.

Tiny plot twist: some mobile browsers block autoplay until someone taps the page. The site tries to start the song after the date; if your browser says "nope," the first tap starts it. Bit of a buzzkill, but that's the rules.

## Fonts, If You're Feeling Fancy

System fonts keep things quick and easy to read. Feeling fancy? Try Palatino or Georgia for the letter, and Avenir Next, SF Pro Display, or Helvetica Neue for the interface.

## One Thing, Though

This is a personal anniversary gift made just for my girlfriend. The notifications are pretend; please do not use this as an actual messaging app. That would be a whole different side quest.
